Privacy First

Privacy Policy for ChordApp

Effective Date: September 30, 2026 • Last Updated: September 30, 2026

Core Promise: ChordApp processes 100% of all audio locally on your device in real-time. No voice or audio recordings are ever stored, recorded, collected, or transmitted over any network.

1. Overview

ChordApp (“we”, “our”, or “the App”) is designed with privacy as a foundational principle. The application is built to help musicians identify acoustic piano chords in real time without compromising their privacy, collecting personal information, or streaming ambient audio to the cloud.

2. Microphone Usage and Audio Processing

ChordApp requests permission to access your device's microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO on Android and NSMicrophoneUsageDescription on iOS) strictly to fulfill the core musical functionality of the application.

Purpose of Access

Microphone access is used solely to capture acoustic piano frequencies from your immediate surroundings to identify musical notes and chord structures in real time.

100% On-Device Neural Processing

Audio is evaluated entirely on your physical smartphone or tablet via local machine learning models (Basic Pitch neural network inference). The audio signal is mathematically processed in real-time to compute pitch activations and chord names without external servers.

Zero Audio Storage

Audio buffers reside exclusively in temporary, volatile random-access memory (RAM) for the few fractions of a second needed for computation and are immediately discarded. ChordApp does not record, save, store, or cache audio files, voice recordings, or sound clips to your device storage or cloud storage.

Zero Network Transmission

Microphone data is never transmitted over the internet, uploaded to remote servers, or shared with third parties. ChordApp functions completely offline without any internet connection.

3. Data Collection and Tracking

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contact lists, photos, or precise location data.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: ChordApp does not include third-party tracking, profiling, or behavioral analytics software.
  • No Advertising: ChordApp does not display advertisements or transmit identifiers to advertising networks.

4. Local Settings Storage

ChordApp saves your preferred user settings (such as Show note names, Keep screen awake, and Sensitivity) locally on your device using standard sandboxed storage (AsyncStorage). This data never leaves your device and is automatically removed if the app is uninstalled.

5. Children's Privacy

ChordApp is completely safe for audiences of all ages, including children. We do not collect or solicit personal information from anyone, compliant with COPPA and GDPR regulations.
